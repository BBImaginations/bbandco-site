Lost in the Woods

BB has lost the path and there are wolves about. Answer a question to unlock the map, then help BB jump the logs, duck the branches and bark the wolves away until he is home.

Play Lost in the Woods

How to play

  1. Pick a question level. Questions change to match.
  2. Answer the first question. It floats over BB's head and unlocks the map.
  3. Tap Walk, then Jump for logs, Duck for branches and Bark when a wolf glows. A warning shows which is coming.
  4. At the end the wolf pack appears: three barks to chase them off, like in the book. One last question and BB is home.

Why we made it

Short and played with one hand, it was made for waiting: a queue, a car park, the last five minutes before dinner. Wrong answers get a hint and a second go, so nobody is stuck, and a bump just stops BB for a moment. Questions range from counting, colours and shapes up to times tables, rhymes and compass points.

Like all our games, it needs no download and no account, collects no personal information, and works in the browser of any phone, tablet or laptop. On a phone you can add it to your home screen from inside the game.
