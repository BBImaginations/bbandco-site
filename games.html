Who Wins?

Someone always shouts "last one up the stairs is the loser!" and, funnily enough, they are never last. Who Wins? fixes that. Pick a challenge, lock the rule, do the challenge, then find out together whether first or last was the winner, the silly sausage, or the one doing the chicken dance. Sometimes the rule says Mum or Dad loses, whatever happened.

  • Four modes: Funny, Winners, Dare and Chaos
  • The rule is chosen and sealed before anyone moves
  • 2 to 10 players, no names to type
  • Works in any browser, nothing to install
