About us

Inspired by two little boys. Created by Mum.

BB & Co Imaginations began with two boys, their big imaginations, and the everyday moments of childhood that somehow turn into the best stories.

Our stories are inspired by real moments from family life: woodland adventures, funny conversations, made-up games, little discoveries and all the wonderful chaos that comes with raising children. As their mum, I started turning those moments into children's books and original songs, adding a little imagination along the way.

Childhood is already full of stories. Sometimes a walk becomes an adventure, a butterfly inspires a song, or a game becomes the beginning of a whole new world.

BB & Co Imaginations is a growing collection of books, music and creative adventures, with more to come. It is made for little imaginations, and for the mums and dads making memories alongside them.

Welcome to BB & Co Imaginations.

Two names, one author

You will see two names on our work. The Magic Pen, Lost in the Woods and Kenya Safari are published under Sonia Goualin; The Great Easter Rescue and the song Butterflies under Sonia Badu-Boateng. They are the same person, Sonia's maiden name and her married name. Future books may settle on one of them, or on a pen name, but everything with either name on it comes from the same kitchen table.
