Who Wins?

Pick a challenge, lock in the rule, then go! Once everyone has finished, reveal the rule to find out who wins. Expect silly surprises, unexpected winners and plenty of laughs.

  • 2–10 players, no names to type
  • 150+ rules across four modes
  • Add to your home screen, works offline
Play Who Wins?

How to play

  1. Type a challenge or tap one of the ideas: run to the sofa, put your shoes on, finish your drink.
  2. Choose a mode: Funny, Winners, Dare or Chaos. Pick the number of players.
  3. Lock the rule. It is chosen at random and sealed before anyone moves.
  4. Do the challenge, then tap Reveal. Sometimes the rule says Mum or Dad wins, or loses, whatever happened.

Why we made it

Someone always shouts 'last one up the stairs is the silly sausage', and somehow they are never last. Who Wins? takes the rule out of the caller's hands: it is picked before the race and cannot be changed after it. Children grasp fairness the moment they see the padlock. The rule might reward first, last or even the middle, so there is no point rushing, which also makes it a calmer game on the stairs.

Like all our games, it needs no download and no account, collects no personal information, and works in the browser of any phone, tablet or laptop. On a phone you can add it to your home screen from inside the game.
