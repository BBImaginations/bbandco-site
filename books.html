The Adventures of Logan and Louis

Four adventures so far, each one a full-colour paperback. Every story is inspired by something our own family has lived through, then given a magical twist.

Christmas

The Magic Pen

Paperback · 28 pages

On a snowy Christmas Eve, Logan and Louis find a pen that makes wishes come true. Snow falls, hot chocolate flows, and the garden fills with surprises, until one wish goes a little too far. A story about family, forgiveness and the joy of sharing.

Adventure

Lost in the Woods

Paperback · 27 pages

When the boys head into the woods to find their dog, BB, an ordinary day becomes an adventure. With courage, teamwork and a few clever clues, they learn that even when you are lost, love and friendship can lead you home.

Travel

Kenya Safari

Paperback · 29 pages

From the busy streets of Nairobi to the wild plains of lions, elephants and giraffes, Logan and Louis discover amazing animals, learn new Swahili words and explore life along the Equator.

Easter

The Great Easter Rescue

Paperback

When all the chocolate in the world disappears, Logan and Louis discover that Easter is about much more than eggs and sweets. With the help of a very special bunny, the brothers are swept into a magical adventure through the woods, where they must face mysterious goblins and make a choice that could change everything. A story about bravery, kindness and the power of giving.
