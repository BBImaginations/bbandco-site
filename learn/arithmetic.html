Learn · Year 6 SATs

Arithmetic practice

The real 2025 arithmetic paper, marked instantly against the official mark scheme. Free, no sign-up, works on a phone.

How to answer
  • Type the number only. Commas are fine: 19648 or 19,648.
  • Fractions as 2/9. Mixed numbers as 1 1/12 (a space between). Equivalent fractions count.
  • Percentage questions want a number, not a percentage sign, exactly as in the real test.
  • Long multiplication and division are worth 2 marks. Work them out on paper, then type the answer.
