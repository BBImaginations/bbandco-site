Where to find us this Christmas

The dates are in. Here is where you can find the BB & Co Imaginations stall this Christmas.

  • Aldershot Christmas Cracker
    Saturday 14 November 2026 · 12:00pm – 5:30pm
  • Farnborough Frost Fair
    Saturday 21 November 2026 · 10:00am – 3:30pm
  • Staines Preparatory School Winter Market
    Saturday 28 November 2026 · 11:30am – 2:30pm
  • Pamber Heath Memorial Hall
    Sunday 29 November 2026 · 1:00pm – 4:00pm

We’ll have all four books in The Adventures of Logan and Louis, including our Christmas story The Magic Pen, and every copy can be signed and dedicated on the day. Buy a book and the children get a spin of our prize wheel, and there will be a tablet on the stall so they can try our free games while you browse.

Our Christmas song, Santa, Where Are You?, is out on 6 November, so we may well be humming it. Come and say hello!

Can’t make it? The books are on Amazon, and you can sign up to hear about the song and anything new.
