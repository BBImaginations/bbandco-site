Christmas is coming to BB & Co 🎄

This Christmas, BB & Co Imaginations is heading to the Christmas markets!

For the first time, you’ll be able to find us at selected Christmas markets and fairs, where we’ll be bringing along our full collection of children’s storybooks, including our very own Christmas adventure.

We’re already dreaming up our festive stall and can’t wait to meet some of the little readers, parents, grandparents and families who are discovering the world of BB & Co.

We’ll be sharing the dates and locations of the Christmas markets we’ll be attending very soon, so watch this space!
