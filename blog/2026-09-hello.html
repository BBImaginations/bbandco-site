Hello from BB & Co Imaginations

Welcome to our corner of the internet. If you have found us through one of the books, thank you for reading. If you have arrived some other way, here is the short version.

We are a family making stories for children aged 4 to 9. So far that means four picture books in The Adventures of Logan and Louis series, one song called Butterflies, and a set of animated short films that are in the works.

This blog is where we will post about new releases, what is happening behind the scenes and the odd thought about reading with children. We will not post often, but when we do it will be worth a look.

Thanks for being here.
